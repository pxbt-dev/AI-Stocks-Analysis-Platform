🧠 AI Model & Indicator Explainer

ML Prediction Engine

▾

Our hybrid AI uses optimized ML models (SVM, Random Forest, & Linear Regression) to analyze 11 core technical indicators, trained on deep market history to identify high-probability price targets.

Trend & Momentum

▾
  • Trend — overall market direction. +0.03+ = strong bullish, −0.03− = strong bearish.
  • Momentum — speed of recent price moves. Positive + uptrend = bullish extension; divergence warns of potential reversal.

Technical Indicators

▾

Our neural network processes 11 specialized features for every prediction:

  • Averages — 5/20-period SMA and 12-period EMA for signal noise filtering.
  • Stats — 20-period Volatility and statistical Z-Score for range analysis.
  • Velocity — 10-period Momentum and Rate of Change (ROC) for price speed.
  • Bands — Real-time Bollinger position tracking institutional accumulation zones.
  • Stability — Multi-period Trend Strength and moving Support/Resistance levels.
  • Dynamics — High-order Price Acceleration to detect early breakout momentum.

Multi-Timeframe Analysis

▾

Cross-verification across 1D, 1W, and 1M timeframes ensures predictions catch major secular trends while filtering out daily market noise.
